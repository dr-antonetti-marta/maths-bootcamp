# Maths Boot Camp for Social Scientists

A free, four-session refresher on the maths behind quantitative social science, from functions and logarithms to matrices, derivatives and probability. Written and taught by [Marta Antonetti](https://github.com/dr-antonetti-marta) at the London School of Economics (2026 edition).

No maths beyond school level assumed. It pairs with my [R bootcamp](https://github.com/dr-antonetti-marta/r-bootcamp): Session 4 calls back to a variance example students first compute in R.

The slides open in your browser. Use the arrow keys to move, **F** for full screen, **Esc** for an overview of all slides. Each deck is a single self-contained file, so you can also download it and open it offline.

Work through the sessions in order: each builds on the last.

## Solutions

The exercise sheets here are the **student versions**, without worked answers, so that solutions don't circulate among future cohorts. If you're an instructor or self-learner and would like the solutions, email me at [m.antonetti@lse.ac.uk](mailto:m.antonetti@lse.ac.uk).
